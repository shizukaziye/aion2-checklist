Sources & how resets work

Resets. The daily list clears the first time you open the page after the daily reset hour. The weekly list clears after the reset hour on the reset day. Both are computed in your browser's local time from the settings tab, so set the hour to match the in-game reset once the global clock is known. Nothing clears while you are mid-session.

dailyPeriod = most recent (today or yesterday) at resetHour:00 weeklyPeriod = most recent resetDay at resetHour:00 on load / each minute: if stored period != current period → clear that list

Launch schedule. Advance Access for Founder's Pack buyers runs Sep 30 to Oct 4, 2026. Free launch is Mon Oct 5, 2026 at 06:00 PDT / 13:00 UTC on Steam and the PURPLE launcher. Korea and Taiwan reset at 05:00 server time (UTC+9 / UTC+8) daily and Wednesday weekly; global has not published its clock, so the first weekly reset here is presumed Wed Oct 7.

Counts. Entry limits come from the KR/TW release and launch-week guides and may shift for global. Edit any cap in the settings tab. Sources disagree on Daily Dungeon (7 vs 14 per week); the higher one is the default.

Item level gates quoted by guides: Expedition Exploration at 1600 (Vakron Sky Island) and 2200 (Ferocious Horn Den), Sanctuary at CP 2700.
